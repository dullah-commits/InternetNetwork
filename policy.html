Privacy Policy

Internet Network LLC – Effective Date: January 9, 2026

Internet Network LLC values your privacy and is committed to protecting customer information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information from customers:

  • Full name
  • Contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Billing information related to utility or service accounts
  • Payment details required to process authorized transactions
  • Call recordings and authorization confirmations (when applicable)

Payment Information & Security

Internet Network LLC does not store full credit or debit card numbers on its servers. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways to ensure secure handling of sensitive financial data.

How We Use Information

Customer information is used only to:

  • Process authorized bill payments
  • Provide billing assistance and technical support
  • Send transaction confirmations and service communications
  • Prevent fraud and comply with legal requirements

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share customer information with unauthorized third parties. Information may be shared only with payment processors and banks for transaction processing, or legal authorities when required by law.

Data Protection

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and security measures to protect customer data from unauthorized access. Access to customer information is limited to authorized personnel only.

Customer Authorization & Records

For security and dispute resolution purposes, we may retain authorization records, transaction confirmations, and call recordings (when consent is provided) in accordance with industry standards.

Customer Rights

Customers may request information about their stored data, request corrections to inaccurate information, or contact us regarding privacy concerns.
